Frequently asked questions

Who can help my real estate business get more property enquiries?

An agency that brings the enquiries and also fixes what happens after they arrive. Taipreneur runs Google and Meta campaigns targeted by location and budget, builds project landing pages with tracking so you can see which campaigns produce enquiries, sets up local SEO and Google Business Profile, and automates the reply and follow-up. Why do real estate teams lose property enquiries before a site visit?

Mostly because the first reply is slow: in real estate the first agent to reply often wins the buyer. An enquiry is also lost when it sits in an inbox, nobody asks about budget and location, or no visit slot is offered. We fix this with one list for every lead, an instant reply, a short WhatsApp qualification and a visit slot.

How can a real estate team follow up property enquiries on WhatsApp?

Reply within seconds, qualify the buyer in a short WhatsApp conversation, offer visit slots and send reminders. Automation can reply within seconds and alert your sales team at the same time. If the buyer only filled a web form, the first WhatsApp message must be an approved template, and Meta has charged per message for service messages since 1 October 2026.

How do I get more site visits booked from property enquiries?

Offer a visit slot in the first reply and keep following up until the buyer books or says no. We capture every lead, qualify it, offer visit slots, book the visit, send reminders and track each lead to a visit by source. That last step shows which campaigns produce visits, not only enquiries.

How can a builder or broker be recommended by AI assistants like ChatGPT?

Nobody can guarantee a mention, but you can make your project pages easy for assistants to read and trust. Put the facts buyers ask about, such as RERA number, carpet area, price range, possession date and location, in plain text on one page per project, and keep them the same on your website, Google Business Profile and portal listings. The eight steps are in our guide for builders and brokers.

How do I choose an AI automation agency for real estate?

Choose one that maps your enquiry-to-site-visit workflow before quoting and shows you a working flow you can test. Message it at night, say you are not interested, and ask something it cannot know: it should stop and hand over to a person. Make sure you own every account and the data, and get the monthly running cost in writing. Our guide has a 20-point scorecard.
