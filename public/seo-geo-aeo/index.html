SEO: Search Engine Optimization

  • Technical audit: crawling, indexing, speed and mobile
  • Keyword and page plan around what your customers search
  • On-page content, titles, headings and internal links
  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile
  • Link building from real, relevant sources

GEO: Generative Engine Optimization

  • Clear, quotable facts about your business that AI systems can cite
  • Consistent business details across the web
  • llms.txt and well-structured pages for AI crawlers
  • Monitoring where AI assistants mention you or your competitors

AEO: Answer Engine Optimization

  • Question-and-answer content that wins featured snippets and voice answers
  • FAQ, Organization, Service and Article schema markup
  • Short direct answers at the top of key pages

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO helps you rank in search results. GEO helps AI assistants understand and recommend you. AEO structures your content to be chosen as the direct answer. They reinforce each other.

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can help within weeks. Meaningful organic growth usually takes a few months and keeps compounding.

Can you guarantee first-page ranking?

No honest agency can. We commit to the work, clear reporting and improving the signals that drive rankings and citations.
