Data Deletion

Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can ask Taipreneur to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. This applies to data from our website and from WhatsApp chats with us.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to tai@taipreneur.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Send it from the address you used with us, or include the WhatsApp number you chatted from.
  2. Or send the message "Delete my data" in our WhatsApp chat.
  3. Tell us your name and the phone number or email we should look for, so we find the right records.

What happens next

  • We confirm we received your request, usually within 2 working days.
  • We delete your conversation history, call summaries, booking records and contact details from our systems within 30 days.
  • We will tell you when it is done.

What we may keep

We may keep a minimal record that you asked us to stop contacting you, so we don't message you again, and any records the law requires us to keep, for example invoices for paid work.

More about what we collect and why is in our Privacy Policy.
