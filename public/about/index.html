About Taipreneur and Abhay Tai

Taipreneur helps colleges, real estate teams and growing businesses get found on Google and in AI answers, and turn enquiries into booked appointments. It is run by Abhay Tai from Bangalore, India.

Book a free callGet a quote

Who runs it

  • Abhay Tai is an AI and automation consultant based in Bangalore, with a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from NIT Karnataka, Surathkal (2026).
  • He works directly on every project: strategy, campaigns, websites and automation.
  • He works with clients in India and the United States.

What he has done

  • Ran SEO, GEO, AEO and Google and Meta advertising for three colleges, managing over INR 6 lakh of ad spend.
  • Built a lead-tracking setup that sends enquiries and offline conversions back to Google and Meta, with an automated WhatsApp reply.
  • Built lead capture and WhatsApp qualification that pulls enquiries from property portals into a CRM for a real estate firm.
  • Built NITKSTAR, a search and question-answering platform adopted by the NITK Alumni Association for over 1,000 alumni.
  • Builds websites and automation for AlphaBetChess, a chess education business in the United States.

How we work
