Best Business Movies for Founders and Salespeople

15 business, sales and finance films ranked for founders: ratings, swearing level, what to learn and where to watch in India. Checked 8 Nov 2026.

Key takeaways
  • Start with Margin Call (107 minutes). It is short, tense and included with Prime Video in India.
  • For sales and persuasion: Glengarry Glen Ross, Air and The Wolf of Wall Street.
  • Four of the 15 (Glengarry Glen Ross, Michael Clayton, Molly's Game, Wall Street) were not on any Indian streaming or rental platform on 8 Nov 2026.
  • Almost all of them swear heavily, and several have graphic violence or sex. Warnings are below.

Good business films compress years of sales, negotiation and deal behaviour into two hours. You see what pressure does to people, which tactics work, and which ones end badly. This is the list I would give a founder or a salesperson who wants to be entertained first and pick up something useful second.

How I ranked them

Each film got two numbers. Fit is my score for how much it delivers on three things: business or dealmaking, thriller tension, and sharp adult dialogue. Quality is the average of its IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores. The final score is 60% fit and 40% quality. I did not rank by popularity or by how much the characters swear.

I only included English-language films from US studios, and no TV series. The chart shows why Glengarry Glen Ross wins: it is the only film that is near the top on both.

Five to watch first, and what each one teaches

This order follows length and availability in India. Glengarry Glen Ross goes last only because you may struggle to find it. None of the notes give away the story.

  1. 1. Margin CallUse: when you escalate a problem, bring the numbers and a clear decision for the person above you.
    Avoid: the film shows people passing risk to others who have not been told.
  2. 2. The Big ShortUse: a contrarian view only holds up if you have read the primary data yourself and tested it against people who disagree.
    Avoid: the film shows institutions selling products they know are weak.
  3. 3. The Social NetworkUse: put co-founder terms and ownership in writing early, while everyone still likes each other.
    Avoid: acting in bad faith toward a partner because the deal was only verbal.
  4. 4. The Wolf of Wall StreetUse: a trained, scripted pitch with rapport and a clear next step converts better than improvising.
    Avoid: the same scripts are used to mislead customers. That is fraud, not sales technique.
  5. 5. Glengarry Glen RossUse: qualify the lead and find the real objection before you pitch features. More on that in the inside sales guide.
    Avoid: the film shows pressure tactics and deception used on customers and colleagues.

Content warnings

  • The Wolf of Wall Street: explicit sex and nudity, heavy drug use.
  • Casino: severe graphic violence.
  • The Departed: severe graphic violence, some nudity.
  • Uncut Gems: moderate violence, some nudity, sustained distress.
  • Nightcrawler: moderate violence and crime-scene aftermath.
  • The Big Short: strip-club nudity. The Social Network and Wall Street: moderate nudity.

These come from the IMDb parents guide severity ratings. The other films have little or none.

How I checked, and what I could not

Limits

Ratings, runtimes and availability were read on 8 Nov 2026 and will change. Streaming catalogues change often, so check the platform before you plan an evening.

Swearing labels are my reading of the sources, not exact counts. Exact counts exist only where a source gave one: 408 for Uncut Gems, 422 for Casino and 237 for The Departed. The Wolf of Wall Street holds the record for a narrative film, with no number in the source I read.

Where to watch comes from JustWatch India. I opened the platform page to confirm Margin Call, Air and The Departed (Prime Video), Uncut Gems (Netflix), The Wolf of Wall Street and Casino (JioHotstar) and the Boiler Room rental. The Social Network on SonyLIV could not be confirmed, and the other rental prices come from JustWatch only.

Indian age certificates are not shown here. I did not check the CBFC database, only IMDb and platform pages, and several were missing or conflicting.

Do this today

Watch Margin Call tonight. Afterwards write one sentence: what did the person who found the problem do to get heard? Then try it in your next message to a client or manager.
