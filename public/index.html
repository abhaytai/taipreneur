What clients say.

Questions we hear often.

What does Taipreneur do?

Taipreneur is a digital agency in Bangalore. We build websites, run digital marketing, automate lead follow-up with AI, and improve visibility on Google and in AI assistants through SEO, GEO and AEO.

What are GEO and AEO?

GEO (generative engine optimization) helps AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity understand and recommend your business. AEO (answer engine optimization) structures your content so it is chosen as the direct answer in featured snippets and voice search.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted after a free 15-minute call, so you know the full cost before work starts.

Which businesses do you work with?

Mostly colleges and institutions, real estate firms, and growing service businesses in India and abroad.

Can you redesign my website without losing Google rankings?

Yes. We keep existing URLs, add redirects where pages move, and check indexing in Search Console before and after launch.
