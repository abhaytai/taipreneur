Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Taipreneur ("we", "us") is a business run by Abhay Tai in India. This policy explains what personal data we handle when you visit taipreneur.com, contact us, or chat with us on WhatsApp, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, phone number, email address and business name.
  • WhatsApp conversations: the messages you send us, the time they were sent, and your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, as provided to us by WhatsApp.
  • Information about your business: your website, lead sources, the problem you want solved, budget, timeline and who decides. We ask these questions so we can understand your problem before a call.
  • Booking details: the date and time of a call you book with us.
  • Website data: basic technical data such as pages visited, device and browser, collected through standard analytics and the contact form.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and keep the conversation going.
  • To book and confirm a call with you and to remind you about it.
  • To prepare a short written summary of your problem for the call.
  • To improve our service and keep it secure.

We message you on WhatsApp only because you messaged us first, and only about your enquiry. We do not send you marketing messages unless you have clearly agreed to receive them. We do not sell your data.

Automated replies

Some replies on our WhatsApp number are written by an AI assistant. It answers common questions, asks the qualifying questions above and helps you book a call. A person (Abhay Tai) reads the summary and can step in at any time. You can ask for a human at any point by saying so in the chat.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the services we need to run this:

  • Meta Platforms / WhatsApp, which delivers WhatsApp messages (WhatsApp Business Platform).
  • AI model provider (OpenAI), which receives the text of your messages to generate replies.
  • Cloud hosting and email providers that store our records and send email.

These providers process data on our behalf and under their own terms. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation records and call summaries for as long as we are working with you or discussing working with you, and delete them within 12 months after the last contact unless you ask us to keep them or the law requires us to. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time (see below).

Your choices and rights

  • Stop messages: send "STOP" on WhatsApp and we will stop messaging you.
  • Access, correct or delete the data we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To delete your data, follow the steps on our data deletion page. To use any other right, email tai@taipreneur.com. We reply within 30 days. We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security

We keep data on access-controlled systems and limit who can read it. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send us passwords, card numbers or government ID numbers in a chat.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
